## 明信片提示

共同提示：Historical scene. The illustrated front of one premium vintage postcard for Typer. Museum stationery, fine printed uneven ink on uncoated fibrous cream card. Flat front-on landscape 148:105. Illustration almost fills a narrow ivory ornamental border. No shadows outside the paper, hands, text, address lines or stamps. This is the art face; writing uses a separate face. Early twentieth-century print character, no gloss.
